How it works

From field sheet to final map in three steps

A single workflow that eliminates the flimsy hand-offs between survey, CAD and GIS software.

1

Capture or Upload

Drop in scanned plans, drone imagery, GPS tracks, KAP/CAD files or DEMs. GeoSuite accepts the formats your office already uses.

2

Process Automatically

Computer vision extracts features, geodetic engines transform coordinates, and ODM builds orthomosaics — without manual steps.

3

Export & Publish

Download results in GPX, KML, GeoJSON, SHP, Excel and PDF, or publish interactive layers directly to the web.
